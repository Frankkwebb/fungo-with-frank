SEALED DUEL

Five fighters. Every stat encrypted. Outplay a rival you can never see, and learn only one bit per round: did you win?

Rules in 30 seconds
  1. Each side holds 5 fighters with an element and 4 stats: Secrecy, Intuition, Will, Energy.
  2. Every round has a public focus stat. A fighter scores its total power plus the focus stat, so that stat counts double.
  3. Elements loop: Night beats Mist, Mist beats Moon, Moon beats Dream, Dream beats Night. A winning matchup adds 15%.
  4. Flash twice per match for +20% power in that round. Everyone sees you spent it.
  5. Each fighter fights once. First to 3 round wins takes the duel. Ties go to higher Will.
  6. Keys: 1 to 5 picks a fighter, F toggles Flash, Enter fights.
