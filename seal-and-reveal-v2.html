04 · Toy FHE

enc(7) + enc(5) = enc(12)

A contract can add numbers it can never read. Try it with a toy additive cipher.

Toy only. Real FHE also multiplies and compares encrypted values, which is what makes private duels possible.

05 · The draw

Nobody picks what you get.

At mint, two random 64-bit values decide your look and your essence. Class odds: Common 50.0%, Rare 30.1%, Ultra Rare 14.8%, Legendary 5.1%.
